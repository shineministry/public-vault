Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 September 2026 · App: Shine Mail Composer (mail-composer-502617) · Domain: https://72oe-v2sx.shine-ministry.com · Contact: shinumaths989@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy describes how Shine Mail Composer ("we", "our", "the App"), a correspondence tool operated by Shine Ministry and Shineil Keith Mathias, collects, uses, and protects information when you use https://72oe-v2sx.shine-ministry.com/email-composer.html. The App helps the owner compose and send designed HTML emails via their own Gmail account.

1. Who we are

Shine Ministry is a family-operated initiative based in Salzburg, Austria / Anif, Austria. The App is published under Google Cloud project mail-composer-502617 as Shine Mail Composer. For any privacy question contact shinumaths989@gmail.com.

2. What Google data the App accesses

The App requests only the following Google OAuth scope, after your explicit consent:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send — Send email on your behalf. Used solely to transmit an email you composed in the App through your Gmail account via the Gmail API.

We do not request read, inbox, contacts, drive, calendar, or profile scopes. We do not access your inbox, labels, attachments, contact lists, or any other Google service.

How the permission is used

  • When you click Send now in the composer, the front-end sends to, subject, body (text), html, attachments to our Cloudflare Worker https://email.shinumaths909.workers.dev/send-email.
  • The Worker obtains a short-lived access_token from Google's OAuth server using the stored refresh_token and then calls gmail.users.messages.send. The email is delivered from your Gmail address.
  • No email content is retained on the Worker after the API call completes (except transient logs for debugging delivery failures, auto-purged within 7 days).

3. What we collect and what we don't

  • We collect: The recipient address, subject, body, and any files you actively attach at send time — only to perform the send you requested.
  • We store locally only: Your recent recipient history and signature preferences are stored in your browser's localStorage (ecRecipientHistoryV1, ecCustomSignaturesV1) for convenience. This never leaves your device unless you send an email.
  • We do NOT collect: Your Gmail password, inbox contents, contacts, browsing history, location, or any personal data beyond what you type into the composer.
  • No third-party analytics or ad trackers are embedded on the composer page.

4. Data retention and storage

  • OAuth tokens: The Worker's refresh_token for shinumaths989@gmail.com is stored encrypted as a Cloudflare Worker secret (not in code or browser). access_tokens live ~1 hour in memory and are not persisted.
  • Sent mail: A copy is kept in your Gmail Sent folder per Gmail's normal behavior. We do not maintain a separate database of sent emails.
  • Attachments: Base64-encoded only for the duration of the API call, then discarded. Files are not saved to R2 or Firestore.

5. How we share data

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties. The only external transfer is to Google's Gmail API (googleapis.com) to deliver the message you authored, and to the recipient mail servers. We do not use the data for AI training, profiling, or marketing.

6. Security

  • All connections use HTTPS/TLS.
  • OAuth tokens are stored as Cloudflare encrypted secrets, never exposed to the browser.
  • The composer page enforces a Content Security Policy and client-side validation; the Worker enforces Origin checks.
  • You can revoke the App's access anytime at myaccount.google.com/permissions → remove Shine Mail Composer. The stored refresh token will then return invalid_grant and sending will be blocked until you re-authorize.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Use the App is entirely optional — you can also use Copy full email or Open in mail app to send via a mailto: link without granting Gmail API scope.
  • Clear browser localStorage to erase recipient history.
  • Revoke OAuth access as described above to stop all Gmail API access.
  • Contact us to request deletion of any transient server logs containing your email address.

8. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes

We will update the Effective date above when this policy changes. Material changes will be noted on the composer page.
